# Halo

Halo shows a first-timer what tonight's aurora will look like from where they are, where to stand, and how to shoot it.

- **Halo Score, 0–50.** One rating from how close the aurora gets, clouds, darkness, and moonlight. It is not a percent chance.
- **Tonight or the May 10, 2024 storm.** Live forecast, or a replay of that G5 night.
- **360° sky.** Drag and zoom a view for your eyes or your camera. Grok Imagine paints it when connected; otherwise Halo draws it.
- **Tonight's plan.** When to go, where to stand, which way and how high to look, what eyes and a camera will see, and a short checklist.
- **Local or API key.** The plan and camera steps can come from Gemma on this machine, or from the Gemini API.
- **Your camera.** Pick a phone or camera and whether you have a tripod. Halo gives the mode, settings, tap-by-tap steps, and what that device cannot change.
- **Hour by hour.** The night at your location, with the best window marked.
- **Aurora map.** A globe of the oval, and a nearby map of scores within about 200 km, with the best spot starred and a directions link.
- **Why tonight.** The flares and solar storms behind the forecast.
